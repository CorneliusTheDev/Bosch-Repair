## Error Code E01 – Heating Element Failure

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E01 Mean?

If your Bosch dryer is displaying the E01 error code, it indicates a **heating element or heater circuit failure**. The heating element is the primary component responsible for generating the hot air that dries your clothes. When the control board detects that the heating element is not drawing current, is drawing too much current, or is not producing the expected temperature rise, it triggers the E01 error code and stops the drying cycle.

This error code is found on Bosch condensation dryers and heat pump dryers sold in the United States, including the 300 Series, 500 Series, and 800 Series models. Bosch dryers sold in the US market are predominantly 240V ventless condensation or heat pump models, and the heating system on these units differs from traditional vented dryers.

When the E01 error appears, your dryer may stop mid-cycle, fail to produce warm air, or refuse to start a heated drying cycle. The drum may still tumble if you select an air-dry (no heat) cycle, which helps confirm that the issue is specific to the heating system.

### Common Causes of the E01 Error Code

Several issues can cause the heating element circuit to fail on your Bosch dryer:

- **Burned-out heating element** – The heating element wire or coil can break or burn through after years of use. When the element breaks, the circuit is open and no heat is produced. This is the most common cause of E01 on older Bosch dryers.
- **Blown thermal fuse** – Bosch dryers have one or more thermal fuses in the heater circuit that blow permanently if the dryer overheats. A blown thermal fuse interrupts the heating circuit completely. Thermal fuses do not reset — once blown, they must be replaced.
- **Defective high-limit thermostat** – The high-limit thermostat monitors the air temperature near the heating element and cuts power if the temperature exceeds a safe threshold. A failed thermostat can cut power prematurely or fail open, preventing the element from receiving power.
- **Heater relay failure on the control board** – The relay on the main control board that switches power to the heating element can fail, preventing the element from being energized even when the control board commands heating.
- **Wiring connection issues** – The high-current wiring connecting the control board to the heating element and thermal protection devices can develop loose connections, corroded terminals, or heat-damaged insulation, especially at the terminal block and element connections.
- **Restricted airflow causing overheating** – Clogged lint filters, blocked condenser units, or restricted internal ducting can cause the dryer to overheat, blowing the thermal fuse and triggering E01.

### How to Troubleshoot the E01 Error Code

Before calling for professional Bosch dryer repair, try these steps:

1. **Clean the lint filter** – Remove and thoroughly clean the lint filter. On Bosch condensation dryers, also clean the condenser unit (located behind the access panel at the bottom front of the dryer) by rinsing it under running water.
2. **Check for airflow restrictions** – Ensure nothing is blocking the air intake vents on the dryer. For vented models, check the external vent hood for blockages.
3. **Reset the dryer** – Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the dryer for at least 60 seconds, then turn it back on. This resets the control board and may clear a temporary electronic fault.
4. **Test with an air-dry cycle** – Run a no-heat or air-fluff cycle to confirm the drum still tumbles. If it does, the motor and drum system are fine, and the problem is isolated to the heating circuit.
5. **Feel the exhaust air** – If the dryer runs but clothes are not drying, feel the air inside the drum during operation. If it is room temperature, the heating element is definitely not working.

### When to Call a Professional

The E01 error typically requires replacement of the heating element, thermal fuse, or high-limit thermostat — components that are located deep inside the dryer and require disassembly to access. Working with 240V circuits is dangerous without proper training. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for safe, professional heating system repair using factory-certified Bosch parts.

## Error Code E02 – NTC Temperature Sensor Fault

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e02-ntc-temperature-sensor-fault/`

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E02 Mean?

The E02 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates an **NTC (Negative Temperature Coefficient) temperature sensor fault**. NTC sensors are thermistors — electronic components whose resistance changes predictably with temperature. Your Bosch dryer uses one or more NTC sensors to monitor air temperature at critical points in the drying system, including the drum inlet, drum outlet, and condenser.

When the control board reads the resistance of an NTC sensor and finds a value that is outside the expected range — extremely high (indicating an open circuit), extremely low (indicating a short circuit), or erratic (indicating an intermittent connection) — it triggers the E02 fault code. The control board needs accurate temperature readings to regulate the heating element and protect your clothes from overheating or under-drying.

This error appears on Bosch dryer models sold in the United States, including condensation dryers, heat pump dryers, and compact ventless dryers. Without functioning temperature sensors, the dryer cannot safely operate because it cannot monitor or control the drying temperature.

### Common Causes of the E02 Error Code

Temperature sensor faults on your Bosch dryer can stem from:

- **Failed NTC sensor** – The thermistor itself can fail due to age, heat cycling fatigue, or moisture damage. An open-circuit failure (infinite resistance) is the most common mode, though short circuits also occur.
- **Disconnected sensor wire** – The thin wires connecting the NTC sensor to the control board can vibrate loose from their connector pins over many drying cycles. This creates an open circuit that the board interprets as a sensor failure.
- **Corroded connector contacts** – The electrical connectors near the NTC sensor are exposed to heat and moisture (especially in condensation dryers) and can corrode, increasing resistance and degrading the temperature signal.
- **Damaged wiring** – Wires can be pinched, chafed, or heat-damaged where they route past the heating element or through other high-temperature areas inside the dryer.
- **Lint buildup on the sensor** – In some dryer designs, lint accumulation directly on the NTC sensor can insulate it from the air stream, causing it to read temperatures incorrectly.
- **Control board input circuit failure** – The analog-to-digital converter or reference circuit on the control board that reads the NTC resistance can fail, causing the board to misinterpret a functional sensor's signal.

### How to Troubleshoot the E02 Error Code

Try these steps to address the temperature sensor fault:

1. **Reset the dryer** – Turn off the circuit breaker for 60 seconds and then turn it back on. A temporary electronic glitch can sometimes cause a false E02 reading.
2. **Clean the lint system thoroughly** – Remove and clean the lint filter, clean the condenser unit (on condensation models), and vacuum any accessible lint from inside the dryer drum and around the door seal area.
3. **Check sensor accessibility** – On some Bosch dryer models, the NTC sensor is visible inside the drum (it looks like a small black or white probe extending into the air stream). If you can see it, check whether it is heavily coated in lint.
4. **Run a test cycle** – After resetting, start a timed dry cycle and observe whether the dryer heats normally. If it runs without showing E02, the error may have been caused by a temporary sensor reading anomaly.
5. **Monitor for intermittent behavior** – If the E02 appears and disappears across multiple cycles, a loose wiring connection is likely. The vibration of the dryer causes the connection to make and break intermittently.

### When to Call a Professional

NTC sensor replacement requires accessing the interior of the dryer and testing the sensor with a multimeter to confirm failure. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for professional temperature sensor diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified Bosch parts.

## Error Code E03 – Drive Motor Fault

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e03-drive-motor-fault/`

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E03 Mean?

The E03 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates a **drive motor fault**. The drive motor rotates the drum that tumbles your clothes during the drying cycle. When the control board commands the motor to run and either receives no feedback from the motor tachometer (speed sensor), detects that the motor is drawing abnormal current, or determines the motor cannot achieve the required speed, it triggers the E03 error.

This error code appears on Bosch condensation and heat pump dryer models sold in the United States. When E03 is displayed, the drum will not turn, and the drying cycle will not proceed. In some cases, you may hear a humming or buzzing sound as the motor attempts to start, while in other cases there is complete silence from the motor area.

The drive motor on a Bosch dryer is a brushless DC motor on newer models or an AC induction motor on older models. Both types are controlled by the motor drive circuit on the main control board.

### Common Causes of the E03 Error Code

Several issues can cause the drive motor to fail on your Bosch dryer:

- **Seized motor bearings** – The motor bearings can wear out and seize over time, especially if the dryer has been in service for many years. Seized bearings prevent the motor shaft from rotating even though the motor is receiving power.
- **Broken or stretched drive belt** – If the belt that wraps around the drum and the motor pulley breaks or slips off, the motor runs freely but the drum doesn't turn. The motor may detect this as an abnormal no-load condition and trigger E03.
- **Overloaded drum** – An extremely heavy, wet load can create too much mechanical resistance for the motor to start. Bosch dryers have specific weight limits, and exceeding them can strain or stall the motor.
- **Jammed drum** – Foreign objects caught between the drum and the dryer housing, or a drum support roller that has seized, can prevent the drum from rotating and stall the motor.
- **Motor winding failure** – The internal copper windings of the motor can develop short circuits or open circuits, especially after overheating events.
- **Motor capacitor failure** – On models with an AC induction motor, the start capacitor is essential for getting the motor turning. A failed capacitor prevents the motor from starting even though the windings are intact.
- **Control board motor driver failure** – The motor drive circuit on the control board can fail, preventing proper power delivery to the motor.

### How to Troubleshoot the E03 Error Code

Try these basic troubleshooting steps:

1. **Reduce the load** – If the dryer was heavily loaded, remove some items to reduce the weight and restart the cycle.
2. **Spin the drum by hand** – Open the dryer door, reach in, and try to spin the drum by hand. It should rotate freely with a slight belt resistance. If it won't turn or feels very stiff, there may be a mechanical obstruction or seized roller.
3. **Listen for motor sounds** – Close the door and start a cycle. Listen carefully — a humming without drum rotation suggests the motor is trying but can't turn. Complete silence suggests the motor is not receiving power.
4. **Reset the dryer** – Turn off the circuit breaker for 60 seconds, then restore power. A temporary electronic fault on the motor control circuit can sometimes be cleared with a reset.
5. **Let the motor cool** – If the dryer has been running continuously and the motor overheated, let it cool for at least 30 minutes before restarting. An internal thermal protector in the motor may have tripped.

### When to Call a Professional

Motor repairs require disassembling the dryer to access the motor, belt, drum rollers, and control board. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for professional motor diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Bosch parts.

## Error Code E04 – Condenser Pump Fault

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e04-condenser-pump-fault/`

**SEO Title:** Bosch Dryer Error Code E04 – Condenser Pump Fault | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Bosch dryer showing E04 error? This indicates a condenser water pump fault. Learn what causes the E04 pump error on your Bosch condensation dryer and how to fix it.

**Keywords:** Bosch dryer error code E04, Bosch dryer condenser pump fault, Bosch dryer water tank full, Bosch dryer E04 fix, Bosch dryer pump not draining, Bosch condensation dryer repair, Bosch dryer repair

---

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E04 Mean?

The E04 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates a **condenser water pump fault**. This error is specific to Bosch condensation (ventless) dryers, which are the predominant type of Bosch dryer sold in the United States. Unlike traditional vented dryers that exhaust moist air outside through a duct, condensation dryers cool the moist air to condense the water, which is then collected in a removable water tank or pumped to a drain connection.

The condenser pump is responsible for moving the collected water from the internal collection reservoir up to the removable water tank at the top of the dryer or out through an external drain hose. When the control board commands the pump to run and either the pump doesn't activate, the water level in the reservoir doesn't decrease, or the pump is drawing abnormal current, the E04 error is triggered.

When E04 appears, the dryer may stop drying to prevent water overflow, or it may display the error at the end of a cycle to alert you that water was not properly pumped.

### Common Causes of the E04 Error Code

The condenser pump can malfunction for several reasons:

- **Clogged pump or pump filter** – Lint that passes through the primary lint filter can accumulate in the condenser pump and its small mesh filter. Over time, this buildup can block water flow or jam the pump impeller.
- **Failed pump motor** – The small electric pump motor can burn out due to age, continuous operation against a clog, or electrical component failure.
- **Full or improperly seated water tank** – If the removable water collection tank at the top of the dryer is full, not properly seated, or has a cracked seal, the pump system cannot function correctly.
- **Blocked drain path** – If your Bosch dryer is connected to an external drain via a hose, that hose can become kinked, clogged, or improperly routed, preventing the pump from moving water out.
- **Air lock in the pump** – Air trapped in the pump housing can prevent the pump from priming and moving water effectively.
- **Wiring or connector issues** – The electrical connections to the pump can corrode or loosen, especially in the humid environment near the condenser system.

### How to Troubleshoot the E04 Error Code

These steps can often resolve the condenser pump fault:

1. **Empty the water tank** – Remove the water collection tank from the top of the dryer, empty it completely, and reseat it firmly. Ensure the tank is properly clicked into position.
2. **Clean the water tank and its filter** – Rinse the tank and any filter screens or float mechanisms inside it under running water to remove lint and residue.
3. **Clean the condenser unit** – Open the condenser access panel (usually at the bottom front of the dryer) and remove the condenser. Rinse it thoroughly under running water to clear lint and debris from its fins. Allow it to dry before reinstalling.
4. **Check the drain hose** – If your dryer is connected to an external drain, inspect the drain hose for kinks, clogs, or improper routing. The hose must slope downward from the dryer to the drain and should not be raised above the pump outlet height.
5. **Reset the dryer** – After cleaning and checking all components, turn off the circuit breaker for 60 seconds and restart.
6. **Run a test cycle** – Load a small amount of laundry and run a full dry cycle. Listen for the pump running during the cycle (a soft humming or buzzing sound from the lower area of the dryer).

### When to Call a Professional

If the E04 persists after cleaning, the condenser pump itself may need replacement. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for professional pump diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified parts.

## Error Code E05 – Lint Filter Blocked

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e05-lint-filter-blocked/`

**SEO Title:** Bosch Dryer Error Code E05 – Lint Filter Blocked | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Bosch dryer showing E05? This means the lint filter is blocked or restricted. Learn how to fix the E05 lint filter error on your Bosch dryer.

**Keywords:** Bosch dryer error code E05, Bosch dryer lint filter blocked, Bosch dryer airflow restricted, Bosch dryer E05 fix, Bosch dryer lint trap clogged, Bosch dryer not drying well, Bosch dryer repair

---

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E05 Mean?

The E05 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates that the **lint filter is blocked or that airflow through the dryer is severely restricted**. Bosch dryers sold in the United States — particularly the condensation and heat pump models — are equipped with airflow sensors that monitor the resistance to air movement through the lint filter and ducting system. When the sensor detects that airflow has dropped below the minimum acceptable level, it triggers the E05 error.

Proper airflow is essential for efficient and safe dryer operation. In a condensation dryer, air must circulate freely through the drum, past the lint filter, through the condenser, and back through the heater to the drum. Any restriction in this path reduces drying efficiency, increases energy consumption, extends cycle times, and can cause overheating that damages both your clothes and the dryer's components.

The E05 is actually one of the more helpful Bosch dryer error codes because it alerts you to a maintenance issue before it becomes a more serious (and more expensive) problem.

### Common Causes of the E05 Error Code

Restricted airflow through your Bosch dryer can be caused by:

- **Heavily clogged lint filter** – The most common cause by far. If the lint filter has not been cleaned after recent cycles or has been inadequately cleaned (lint stuck in the mesh fibers), airflow will be significantly restricted.
- **Lint buildup in the filter housing** – Even when you clean the lint filter regularly, fine lint particles can accumulate in the filter housing slot and the ductwork immediately beyond the filter. Over time, this buildup restricts airflow even with a clean filter.
- **Clogged condenser** – On condensation dryers, the condenser unit collects lint that passes through the primary filter. If the condenser fins become clogged with lint, airflow through the entire system is restricted.
- **Blocked exhaust vent** – On vented Bosch dryer models, a clogged or crushed exterior vent duct restricts the dryer's ability to exhaust moist air. Lint buildup in the vent pipe is a common problem, especially in long duct runs.
- **Damaged or missing lint filter** – A filter that is torn, warped, or missing entirely allows lint to bypass the filter and accumulate in the ducting and condenser, creating restrictions further downstream.
- **Dryer sheet residue on filter** – Fabric softener dryer sheets leave an invisible waxy residue on the lint filter mesh over time. This residue blocks the tiny mesh openings and restricts airflow even when no visible lint is present.

### How to Troubleshoot the E05 Error Code

The E05 is usually resolved with thorough cleaning:

1. **Clean the lint filter** – Remove the lint filter and peel off all lint. Then wash the filter in warm soapy water with a soft brush to remove invisible dryer sheet residue. Allow it to dry completely before reinstalling.
2. **Vacuum the filter housing** – Use a vacuum cleaner with a narrow attachment to clean lint from inside the filter slot and the ductwork you can reach through the filter opening.
3. **Clean the condenser** – Remove the condenser unit from the dryer (it pulls out from behind a bottom access panel). Rinse it under running water, flushing both sides, until all lint is removed from the fins. Let it dry before reinstalling.
4. **Check the exhaust vent** – For vented models, disconnect the vent hose from the back of the dryer and check for lint buildup. Also check the exterior vent hood for obstructions (lint, debris, bird nests).
5. **Reset and test** – After thorough cleaning, turn off the circuit breaker for 30 seconds, then restart the dryer with a small load.

### When to Call a Professional

If the E05 persists after thorough cleaning of all lint paths, the airflow sensor itself may be faulty or there may be a deep internal lint blockage. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for professional duct cleaning and sensor inspection.

## Error Code E06 – Door Switch or Latch Fault

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e06-door-switch-latch-fault/`

**SEO Title:** Bosch Dryer Error Code E06 – Door Switch Fault | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Bosch dryer displaying E06? This indicates a door switch or latch fault. Learn the causes and how to fix the E06 door error on your Bosch dryer.

**Keywords:** Bosch dryer error code E06, Bosch dryer door switch fault, Bosch dryer door latch broken, Bosch dryer E06 fix, Bosch dryer won't start, Bosch dryer door not closing, Bosch dryer repair

---

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E06 Mean?

The E06 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates a **door switch or door latch fault**. The door switch is a safety device that tells the control board whether the dryer door is open or closed. The dryer will not operate with the door open — this prevents injury from the rotating drum and exposure to hot air. When the control board does not receive a "door closed" signal from the switch, or when the signal is intermittent, it displays the E06 error.

This error is found on all Bosch dryer models sold in the United States. The E06 can appear when you press Start with the door seemingly closed, or it may appear mid-cycle if the door switch loses its signal during operation (which also immediately stops the drum).

Bosch dryers use a simple mechanical door switch — when the door is pushed closed, a strike on the door depresses a spring-loaded button switch on the dryer frame. The switch completes the circuit that tells the control board the door is secure.

### Common Causes of the E06 Error Code

The door switch or latch can fail for several reasons:

- **Worn or broken door switch** – The mechanical switch has a limited lifespan of button-press cycles. After many years of use, the internal contacts can wear out, corrode, or become stuck.
- **Broken door latch or catch** – The plastic catch mechanism on the door or the strike plate on the dryer frame can crack or break, preventing the door from engaging the switch.
- **Misaligned door** – If the door hinges are loose or the dryer has been moved roughly, the door may not align properly with the switch mechanism. The door closes but doesn't press the switch firmly enough.
- **Lint or debris in the latch area** – Lint accumulation around the latch mechanism can prevent the door from closing fully or prevent the switch from being depressed completely.
- **Wiring issues** – The wires connecting the door switch to the control board can break or develop loose connections, especially at the switch terminals.
- **Door seal interference** – A swollen or displaced door gasket can create enough resistance to prevent the door from closing the final millimeter needed to engage the switch.

### How to Troubleshoot the E06 Error Code

Try these steps to resolve the door switch error:

1. **Close the door firmly** – Open the door fully and close it again with a firm, even push. Listen for a solid click that confirms the latch has engaged.
2. **Clean the latch area** – Wipe the door latch, catch, and switch area with a damp cloth to remove lint, fabric softener residue, and debris.
3. **Inspect the catch mechanism** – Look at the door catch hook and the strike area on the dryer frame. Check for visible cracks, breaks, or misalignment.
4. **Check the door seal** – Examine the door gasket for any areas that are swollen, displaced, or bunched up.
5. **Test the switch** – Close the door and listen for the interior light to turn off (on models with a drum light). If the light stays on with the door closed, the switch is not detecting closure.
6. **Reset the dryer** – Turn off the circuit breaker for 60 seconds, then try starting a cycle.

### When to Call a Professional

If the E06 persists with the door firmly closed, the door switch needs replacement. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for professional door switch diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified Bosch parts.

## Error Code E07 – Condenser Unit Needs Cleaning

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e07-condenser-unit-needs-cleaning/`

**SEO Title:** Bosch Dryer Error Code E07 – Clean Condenser | Causes & Fixes

**Keywords:** Bosch dryer error code E07, Bosch dryer condenser cleaning, Bosch dryer condenser clogged, Bosch dryer E07 fix, Bosch condensation dryer maintenance, Bosch dryer not drying, Bosch dryer repair

---

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E07 Mean?

The E07 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates that the **condenser unit requires cleaning**. This error is specific to Bosch condensation (ventless) and heat pump dryers — the types most commonly sold in the United States market. The condenser is a heat exchanger that cools the warm, moist air from the drum, causing the moisture to condense into water that is collected and pumped away. Over time, fine lint particles that pass through the primary lint filter accumulate on the condenser's fins, reducing its efficiency.

Bosch dryers monitor condenser performance through temperature and humidity sensors. When the condenser becomes sufficiently blocked that drying efficiency drops below acceptable levels, the control board triggers the E07 code. Think of it as a maintenance reminder — the dryer is telling you it needs cleaning to operate efficiently.

The E07 is an important code to address promptly. An ignored E07 leads to progressively longer drying times, higher energy consumption, potential overheating, and eventually more serious error codes like E01 (heating element failure due to overheating) or E05 (complete airflow blockage).

### Common Causes of the E07 Error Code

The condenser becomes dirty due to:

- **Normal lint accumulation** – Even with regular lint filter cleaning, fine lint particles pass through the filter mesh and collect on the condenser fins over many drying cycles. This is normal and expected.
- **Infrequent condenser cleaning** – Bosch recommends cleaning the condenser every 1-3 months depending on usage. Many owners are unaware of this maintenance requirement.
- **Using the dryer without the lint filter** – If the lint filter was removed or damaged, large amounts of lint reach the condenser much faster than normal.
- **Fabric softener residue** – Dryer sheets leave a waxy residue on the lint filter that allows more fine particles to pass through to the condenser.
- **Pet hair** – Households with pets generate significantly more fine fiber material that accelerates condenser fouling.
- **Hard water mineral deposits** – In areas with hard water, mineral deposits from the condensed water can build up on the condenser fins over time, combining with lint to create a stubborn coating.

### How to Troubleshoot the E07 Error Code

The E07 is resolved by cleaning the condenser:

1. **Locate the condenser** – The condenser is accessed through a panel at the bottom front of the dryer. On most Bosch models, release the panel clips or screws and pull the panel down or off.
2. **Remove the condenser** – The condenser slides out of the dryer after releasing its retaining clips or handles. Pull it straight out carefully — water may drip from it.
3. **Clean the condenser thoroughly** – Rinse the condenser under running water in a sink or bathtub. Use the spray nozzle to flush water through the fins from both sides. You should see lint and dirty water wash away. For stubborn buildup, soak the condenser in warm water for 10-15 minutes before rinsing.
4. **Clean the condenser housing** – While the condenser is out, vacuum any lint from the housing cavity inside the dryer using a narrow vacuum attachment.
5. **Dry and reinstall** – Shake excess water from the condenser, let it air-dry for 15-20 minutes, then slide it back into the dryer and secure the retaining clips.
6. **Also clean the lint filter** – Wash the lint filter in warm soapy water to remove any invisible residue, and clean the filter housing with a vacuum.
7. **Reset and test** – Close the access panel, turn on the dryer, and run a cycle. The E07 should clear.

### When to Call a Professional

If the E07 persists after thorough condenser cleaning, the condenser performance sensor may be faulty. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for sensor testing and replacement.

## Error Code E08 – Heat Pump System Fault

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/e08-heat-pump-system-fault/`

**SEO Title:** Bosch Dryer Error Code E08 – Heat Pump Fault | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Bosch dryer showing E08 error code? This indicates a heat pump system fault. Learn the causes and solutions for the E08 error on your Bosch heat pump dryer.

**Keywords:** Bosch dryer error code E08, Bosch heat pump dryer fault, Bosch dryer E08 fix, Bosch dryer heat pump failure, Bosch dryer compressor error, Bosch heat pump dryer repair, Bosch dryer repair

---

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E08 Mean?

The E08 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates a **heat pump system fault**. This error is specific to Bosch heat pump dryers, which are the most energy-efficient dryers in the Bosch lineup and are increasingly common in the United States market. Heat pump dryers work differently from conventional dryers — instead of using a resistive heating element, they use a refrigerant-based heat pump (similar to an air conditioner running in reverse) to heat the drying air and recover the energy from the moisture.

The E08 error means the control board has detected a problem with the heat pump's refrigerant circuit, compressor, or associated sensors. This could mean the compressor is not running, the refrigerant pressures are abnormal, the evaporator or condenser temperatures are out of range, or the heat pump is not producing the expected heat output.

When E08 appears, the dryer will either stop completely or may attempt to continue drying at reduced efficiency. Clothes may come out damp even after a full cycle length, or the dryer may refuse to start at all.

### Common Causes of the E08 Error Code

Heat pump system faults can result from:

- **Compressor failure** – The compressor is the heart of the heat pump system. It can fail due to worn internal bearings, seized pistons, or electrical winding damage. Compressor failure is the most serious and costly repair for a heat pump dryer.
- **Refrigerant leak** – The sealed refrigerant circuit can develop leaks at brazed joints, service ports, or through corrosion damage. Lost refrigerant reduces the heat pump's ability to transfer heat.
- **Clogged condenser or evaporator coils** – Heavy lint accumulation on the evaporator or condenser coils within the heat pump system restricts airflow and can cause the refrigerant pressures to go out of range.
- **Defective heat pump temperature sensor** – Multiple NTC sensors monitor temperatures at various points in the heat pump circuit. A failed sensor gives the control board incorrect information, triggering E08.
- **Fan motor failure** – The heat pump system has dedicated fans to move air across the evaporator and condenser. A failed fan motor disrupts the heat exchange process.
- **Start relay or capacitor failure** – The compressor relies on a start relay and/or run capacitor to begin operation. If these components fail, the compressor cannot start.
- **Ambient temperature too low** – Heat pump dryers have minimum operating temperature requirements (typically above 40°F / 5°C). Operating in a very cold garage or basement can trigger E08.

### How to Troubleshoot the E08 Error Code

Limited DIY troubleshooting is available for heat pump issues:

1. **Check the ambient temperature** – If the dryer is in an unheated space, verify the room temperature is above the minimum operating range (check your owner's manual — usually 40-95°F or 5-35°C).
2. **Clean the condenser** – A clogged condenser can cause the heat pump to malfunction. Remove and thoroughly clean the condenser under running water.
3. **Clean the lint filter** – Ensure the lint filter is clean and the filter housing is free of debris.
4. **Reset the dryer** – Turn off the circuit breaker for five minutes (longer than normal to allow the compressor to equalize pressures) before restarting.
5. **Listen for the compressor** – When the dryer starts, listen for a low humming from the compressor (located in the lower area of the dryer). If you hear clicking followed by silence (repeated attempts to start), the compressor start relay or capacitor may be failing.

### When to Call a Professional

The heat pump system contains sealed refrigerant and requires EPA-certified technicians for service. Do not attempt to open any refrigerant lines or service the compressor yourself. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for professional heat pump diagnosis and repair.

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code E09 Mean?

The E09 error code on a Bosch dryer indicates an **electronic module or auxiliary system error**. This is a general fault code that covers various secondary electronic systems within the dryer that are not classified under more specific error codes. The E09 can relate to the user interface (display) board, an internal communication fault between boards, a drum illumination system failure, or a sensor calibration error.

This error code appears on Bosch dryers sold in the United States, particularly on newer models with advanced electronic controls and multiple circuit boards. When E09 is displayed, the dryer may function partially (the drum may still tumble and heat), but the control system has detected an anomaly that it cannot categorize under the more specific E01 through E08 codes.

The E09 is sometimes described as a "catch-all" electronic error, which means diagnosing the specific component responsible requires either the dryer's built-in diagnostic mode or professional testing with a multimeter.

### Common Causes of the E09 Error Code

An electronic module error on your Bosch dryer can stem from:

- **User interface board failure** – The control panel board that processes your button presses and drives the display can develop component failures, causing erratic behavior or communication errors with the main control board.
- **Communication bus fault** – The data line connecting the user interface board to the main control board can be disrupted by a damaged wire, loose connector, or electromagnetic interference from nearby equipment.
- **Drum light circuit fault** – On models with LED drum illumination, a shorted or open LED circuit can generate an E09 error because the control board monitors auxiliary loads.
- **Power supply irregularity** – Voltage fluctuations or brief power interruptions can corrupt data in the control board's volatile memory, causing the board to enter a fault state.
- **EEPROM data corruption** – The non-volatile memory chip on the control board stores calibration data and cycle parameters. Corruption of this data (from power surges or age) can trigger E09.
- **Software glitch** – Like any computer system, the dryer's control software can occasionally experience runtime errors that trigger fault codes.

### How to Troubleshoot the E09 Error Code

Try these steps to clear the electronic module error:

1. **Power cycle the dryer** – Turn off the circuit breaker for at least three minutes. This extended off period allows all electronic modules to fully discharge and reinitialize when power is restored.
2. **Check the display** – After restarting, observe whether the display shows all segments correctly and responds to button presses. Garbled, flickering, or unresponsive displays suggest a user interface board issue.
3. **Check for power supply issues** – Ensure the dryer is plugged directly into a properly grounded wall outlet (not an extension cord). Verify the outlet provides stable voltage.
4. **Look for recent power events** – If the E09 appeared after a power outage or storm, the control board may have experienced a transient voltage spike.
5. **Try running a cycle** – After reset, start a normal drying cycle. If the dryer operates normally without the E09 returning, the error was likely a one-time software glitch.
6. **Monitor for recurrence** – If E09 returns within a few cycles, note when it appears (start of cycle, mid-cycle, end of cycle) and what the dryer was doing. This information helps a technician narrow down the fault.

### When to Call a Professional

Persistent E09 errors require professional diagnosis with Bosch-specific diagnostic tools to identify which electronic module has failed. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for expert electronic system diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Bosch parts.

## Error Code d02 – Water Tank Full

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code d02 Mean?

The d02 error code on a Bosch dryer means the **water collection tank is full and must be emptied**. This code is specific to Bosch condensation (ventless) dryers, which are the primary type of Bosch dryer available in the United States. Instead of venting moist air outdoors, condensation dryers extract the moisture from your clothes and collect it as liquid water in a removable tank, typically located at the top of the dryer.

When the water level in the tank reaches its maximum capacity, a float switch triggers the d02 code, and the dryer pauses the cycle until the tank is emptied. This is a normal maintenance alert — not a malfunction. The d02 will appear regularly during normal operation, especially when drying large or very wet loads, or when running multiple back-to-back drying cycles.

The frequency of d02 depends on several factors: the size of the load, how wet the clothes are when they enter the dryer, the selected drying program, and whether your Bosch dryer is connected to an external drain (which eliminates the need to empty the tank manually).

### Common Causes of the d02 Error Code

The d02 code appears for these reasons:

- **Tank is genuinely full** – The most common and normal cause. A full load of wet laundry can produce 2-3 liters of water, and the collection tank has a finite capacity.
- **Tank not emptied between cycles** – If you run back-to-back drying cycles without emptying the tank, the second cycle will likely trigger d02.
- **Tank float switch stuck** – In rare cases, the float switch that detects the water level in the tank can stick in the "full" position due to mineral deposits or debris, causing d02 to appear even with an empty or partially full tank.
- **Improperly seated tank** – If the water tank is not pushed fully into its slot, the water inlet tube may not align correctly with the tank opening, causing water to back up.
- **External drain hose clogged** – If your dryer is connected to an external drain via a hose, a clogged or kinked hose prevents water from draining and causes the internal reservoir to overflow, triggering d02.
- **Condenser pump malfunction** – If the pump that moves water from the internal reservoir to the tank (or external drain) is weak or failing, water accumulates faster than it can be pumped out.

### How to Troubleshoot the d02 Error Code

The d02 is typically easy to resolve:

1. **Empty the water tank** – Pull out the water collection tank from the top of the dryer, pour out the water, and reinsert the tank firmly until it clicks into place.
2. **Verify the tank is seated properly** – Push the tank all the way in. If it is not properly seated, the fill tube may not align correctly, preventing water from entering the tank and causing it to back up.
3. **Check for float switch issues** – Look inside the tank for the float mechanism (a small floating piece that rises with the water level). Make sure it moves freely up and down. If it appears stuck, rinse the tank and float area with warm water.
4. **Check the external drain hose** – If your dryer is plumbed to a drain, inspect the external drain hose for kinks, clogs, or improper routing. The hose should slope downward from the dryer to the drain.
5. **Resume the cycle** – After emptying the tank, close the dryer door and press Start to resume the drying cycle.

### When to Call a Professional

If the d02 appears with an empty tank or immediately after emptying, the float switch or condenser pump may need service. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for diagnosis using factory-certified Bosch parts.

### What Does Bosch Dryer Error Code d04 Mean?

The d04 code on a Bosch dryer is a **condenser filter cleaning reminder**, not a fault code. This code appears at regular intervals (typically every 20-30 cycles, depending on the model) to remind you to clean the condenser unit. On some Bosch dryer models, d04 may display as a flashing indicator light rather than a numeric code.

Bosch condensation and heat pump dryers sold in the United States rely heavily on a clean condenser for efficient operation. The condenser is where warm, moist air from the drum is cooled so that moisture can be extracted. Fine lint particles that pass through the primary lint filter gradually accumulate on the condenser's fins, reducing heat transfer efficiency and extending drying times.

Unlike error codes that prevent the dryer from operating, the d04 reminder does not stop the dryer. You can continue to use the dryer, but performance will gradually decline if the condenser is not cleaned. Eventually, a neglected condenser will trigger more serious codes like E05 (airflow blocked) or E07 (condenser needs cleaning — which does affect operation).

### Why Regular Condenser Cleaning Matters

Maintaining a clean condenser on your Bosch dryer provides several important benefits:

- **Faster drying times** – A clean condenser exchanges heat efficiently, meaning cycles complete in the expected time frame. A clogged condenser can double your drying time.
- **Lower energy consumption** – When the condenser is clean, the dryer doesn't have to work as hard or run as long to dry your clothes, saving significant energy over time.
- **Extended appliance life** – Proper airflow prevents overheating of the heating element, compressor (on heat pump models), and other components, extending their service life.
- **Prevent more serious errors** – Regular condenser cleaning prevents the progression from a simple d04 reminder to E05 and E07 fault codes that actually interrupt drying.
- **Better drying results** – Clothes dried in a well-maintained dryer come out evenly dry without damp spots, wrinkles from extended tumbling, or heat damage from compensating.

### How to Clean the Condenser and Clear d04

Follow these steps for a thorough condenser cleaning:

1. **Turn off and unplug the dryer** – Safety first. Make sure the dryer is cool before accessing the condenser.
2. **Locate the condenser access panel** – On most Bosch dryers, the condenser access panel is at the bottom front of the dryer. It may have clips, a latch, or screws holding it in place.
3. **Remove the condenser** – Release the retaining clips or lever and carefully slide the condenser out. It may be wet and may have lint visible on its surface.
4. **Rinse under running water** – Take the condenser to a sink, bathtub, or garden hose and rinse both sides thoroughly under running water. Flush water through the fins from alternating sides until the water runs clear.
5. **Clean the condenser housing** – While the condenser is out, use a vacuum with a narrow attachment to clean any accumulated lint from the housing cavity inside the dryer.
6. **Allow to dry** – Shake excess water off the condenser and let it air-dry for 15-20 minutes. You can also pat it with a clean towel.
7. **Reinstall** – Slide the condenser back into the housing and secure the retaining clips. Replace the access panel.
8. **Reset the reminder** – On some models, the d04 reminder resets automatically after a power cycle. On others, you may need to press and hold a specific button (consult your owner's manual). Turning the dryer off and back on at the circuit breaker usually clears the reminder.

### Setting Up a Cleaning Schedule

To keep your Bosch dryer in optimal condition, establish a regular maintenance routine:

- **After every load:** Clean the primary lint filter
- **Monthly:** Clean the condenser (more often if you have pets or dry heavy loads frequently)
- **Every 3-6 months:** Deep clean the lint filter housing with a vacuum, inspect the door seal, and wipe down the drum interior

If your Bosch dryer continues to display d04 after cleaning and resetting, or if cleaning the condenser doesn't improve drying performance, there may be a deeper issue with the airflow system or sensors. **Contact our Bosch dryer repair team** for a comprehensive dryer inspection and maintenance service.
