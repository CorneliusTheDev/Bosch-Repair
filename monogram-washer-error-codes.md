## Error Code E22 – Fill Timeout / Water Supply Problem

### What Does Monogram Washer Error Code E22 Mean?

The E22 error code on a Monogram washing machine indicates a **fill timeout** — the washer was unable to fill with water within the expected time limit (typically 8 minutes). When the control board activates the water inlet valve to fill the drum, it monitors the water level sensor. If the water level does not rise to the required level within the fill timeout window, E22 is triggered and the cycle stops.

When E22 appears, you may notice that the drum has little or no water in it, or that filling started but stopped before the drum reached the proper water level. This error code is common on Monogram front-load and top-load washers that use GE's electronic control platform.

### Common Causes of the E22 Error Code

- **Water supply valves not fully open** – The hot and cold water supply valves behind the washer may be partially or completely closed, restricting water flow.
- **Kinked or clogged fill hoses** – The rubber hoses connecting the washer to the water supply valves can become kinked or internally clogged with sediment and mineral deposits.
- **Clogged inlet valve screens** – The water inlet valve on the back of the washer has small mesh screens that filter debris from the water supply. These screens can become clogged with mineral deposits, rust, or sediment, severely restricting flow.
- **Failed water inlet valve** – The solenoid-operated inlet valve can fail electrically or mechanically, preventing it from opening to allow water in.
- **Low water pressure** – Monogram washers require a minimum water pressure (typically 20-120 PSI) to fill within the allowed time. Low house water pressure can cause fill timeout.
- **Failed water level pressure sensor** – If the pressure sensor that measures water level in the drum gives incorrect readings, the washer may continue filling and time out, or may register the fill as incomplete even with adequate water.

### How to Troubleshoot the E22 Error Code

1. **Check water supply valves** – Locate the hot and cold water valves behind the washer (usually on the wall). Ensure both are turned fully counterclockwise (open). Many E22 errors are caused simply by a valve that was partially closed during installation or maintenance.
2. **Check for kinked hoses** – Inspect the fill hoses from the wall valves to the back of the washer. Straighten any kinks.
3. **Clean the inlet valve screens** – Turn off both water supply valves, then disconnect the fill hoses from the back of the washer. You will see two small mesh screens inside the valve ports. Use needle-nose pliers to carefully remove them and rinse under running water to clear debris, then reinstall.
4. **Test water pressure** – If you suspect low water pressure, check if other fixtures in your home have strong flow. A whole-house water pressure issue may need to be addressed.
5. **Reset the washer** – Unplug for 60 seconds, plug back in, and attempt a new cycle.

### When to Call a Professional

If E22 persists after checking valves, hoses, and cleaning screens, the water inlet valve or water level pressure sensor may need to be replaced. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for professional diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Code E23 – Flood Protection Drain Activated

### What Does Monogram Washer Error Code E23 Mean?

The E23 error code on a Monogram washing machine indicates that the **flood protection system has been activated** — the washer detected an excessive amount of water in the drum or tub, triggering the safety drain to remove water and prevent overflow. This error is triggered when the water level sensor detects a water level significantly above what was requested by the selected wash cycle.

When E23 appears, the washer stops filling, activates the drain pump to remove excess water, and halts the cycle.

### Common Causes of the E23 Error Code

- **Faulty water inlet valve stuck open** – The most common cause. If the solenoid-operated inlet valve fails in the open position, water continuously trickles or flows into the drum even when the washer is not actively filling. This can happen while the washer is idle between cycles.
- **Failed water level pressure sensor** – A pressure sensor that reads incorrectly may tell the control board the drum is nearly empty when it actually has the correct amount of water, causing the washer to keep filling until the flood protection triggers.
- **Siphoning through drain hose** – If the drain hose is inserted too deeply into the standpipe or is located below the washer's minimum drain height, a siphon effect can pull water back into the tub.

### How to Troubleshoot the E23 Error Code

1. **Check the inlet valve** – After a wash cycle, turn off the water supply valves and observe whether water continues to drip into the drum. Ongoing dripping with the valve closed confirms the valve is stuck open.
2. **Check drain hose position** – Ensure the drain hose is inserted only 6-8 inches into the standpipe and is at the correct height. Consult your Monogram washer installation guide for minimum drain height requirements.
3. **Reset the washer** – Unplug for 60 seconds. The drain pump should have already removed excess water. After reset, run a short cycle and monitor.

### When to Call a Professional

If the inlet valve is dripping continuously or the water level sensor provides incorrect readings, professional diagnosis and component replacement is needed. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for expert repair using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Code E30 – No Drain Pump Signal

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/washer/e30-no-drain-pump-signal/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Washer Error Code E30 – No Drain Pump Signal | Causes & Fixes

### What Does Monogram Washer Error Code E30 Mean?

The E30 error code on a Monogram washing machine indicates that the **control board is not receiving a proper signal from the drain pump**. This is distinct from a drain timeout (E31) — E30 indicates a communication or electrical issue with the pump circuit rather than simply slow drainage. The control board sends a command to the drain pump and monitors the pump's response signal. When this signal is absent or incorrect, E30 is triggered.

### Common Causes of the E30 Error Code

- **Loose electrical connection to the drain pump** – The wiring connector to the drain pump motor has come loose or partially disconnected.
- **Clogged drain pump** – A severely clogged pump impeller can prevent the motor from spinning, causing it to draw excessive current and potentially trip an internal thermal protector.
- **Failed drain pump motor** – The pump motor has burned out electrically.
- **Control board fault** – The control board's pump drive circuit has failed.

### How to Troubleshoot the E30 Error Code

1. **Clean the drain pump filter** – Locate the access panel at the lower front of the washer. Place towels down, slowly open the filter cap, and remove any debris, lint, or foreign objects from the filter and pump cavity.
2. **Check pump electrical connections** – If accessible, ensure the wiring harness connector to the drain pump is firmly seated.
3. **Reset the washer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry a Drain & Spin cycle.
4. **Listen for pump operation** – When drain should occur, listen for the hum of the pump motor. Silence suggests no power to the pump.

### When to Call a Professional

If E30 persists after cleaning the filter, the drain pump electrical connections or pump motor require professional inspection and likely replacement. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for expert drain pump diagnosis and repair.

## Error Code E31 – Drain Timeout / Slow Drain

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/washer/e31-drain-timeout-slow-drain/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Washer Error Code E31 – Drain Timeout | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram washer showing E31 error? This indicates the drain cycle took too long. Learn causes and how to fix E31 on your Monogram washing machine.

**Keywords:** Monogram washer error code E31, Monogram washing machine slow drain, Monogram washer E31 fix, Monogram washer standing water, Monogram washer repair

---

### What Does Monogram Washer Error Code E31 Mean?

The E31 error code on a Monogram washing machine indicates that the **drain cycle exceeded the maximum allowed time** — water is draining too slowly. The control board activates the drain pump and monitors the water level sensor. If the water level does not drop to the expected level within the drain timeout window, E31 is triggered.

When E31 appears, you will find standing water remaining in the wash tub and the washer stopped. E31 is one of the most commonly encountered Monogram washer errors, and many causes can be resolved by the homeowner.

### Common Causes of the E31 Error Code

- **Clogged drain pump filter** – The debris filter (coin trap) at the lower front of the machine catches coins, buttons, hair ties, and lint. When clogged, water cannot flow through efficiently.
- **Blocked or kinked drain hose** – The drain hose can become kinked, crushed, or internally blocked with lint and debris.
- **Drain pump failure** – A weak or worn pump may move water but not fast enough to meet the drain time requirement.
- **Foreign object in the pump impeller** – Small items bypassing the filter can wedge in the pump impeller, restricting rotation.
- **Excessive suds** – Using too much detergent or non-HE detergent creates foam that the pump cannot clear efficiently.
- **Drain standpipe too high** – If the drain standpipe is too high, the pump must work harder to overcome the head pressure.

### How to Troubleshoot the E31 Error Code

1. **Clean the drain pump filter** – Place towels and a shallow pan under the filter access panel (lower front). Slowly open the filter cap and drain water. Remove the filter and clean all debris thoroughly. This is the most common fix for E31.
2. **Check the drain hose** – Ensure the drain hose is not kinked. The hose should be inserted only 6-8 inches into the standpipe and must not be inserted too deeply, which can create a siphon effect.
3. **Use HE detergent** – Monogram front-load washers require HE (High Efficiency) detergent used in the correct quantity. Switch to HE detergent if using regular detergent.
4. **Run a Drain & Spin cycle** – After cleaning the filter, run a Drain & Spin cycle to test drainage.

### When to Call a Professional

If E31 persists after cleaning the filter and checking the drain hose, the drain pump or its impeller may need replacement. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for professional drain pump diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Codes E42 / E45 – Drive Motor Error

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/washer/e42-e45-drive-motor-error/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Washer Error Codes E42/E45 – Drive Motor Error | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram washer showing E42 or E45 error? This indicates a drive motor problem. Learn causes and how to fix E42/E45 on your Monogram washing machine.

**Keywords:** Monogram washer error code E42, Monogram washer E45, Monogram washing machine motor error, Monogram washer E42 fix, Monogram washer not spinning, Monogram washer repair

### What Does Monogram Washer Error Code E42 or E45 Mean?

The E42 and E45 error codes on a Monogram washing machine indicate **drive motor errors** — the motor or motor drive system is operating outside design limits. The control board monitors motor current, speed, and the inverter drive's feedback signals. E42 typically indicates the motor drive is operating above design limits (overcurrent or overspeed), while E45 indicates a related motor control fault.

When these errors appear, the washer may stop mid-cycle, fail to spin, or produce grinding or straining sounds before stopping.

### Common Causes of the E42 / E45 Error Codes

- **Overloaded drum** – Exceeding the washer's rated load capacity forces the motor to work harder than its rated limits, triggering overcurrent protection.
- **Worn motor bearings** – Bearings that have degraded create mechanical drag on the motor, increasing current draw.
- **Failed inverter board** – The motor speed control inverter (on inverter-drive models) has a component failure.
- **Worn drive motor** – Internal electrical failure of the motor windings.
- **Loose motor wiring** – Intermittent connection causing erratic motor control.

### How to Troubleshoot the E42 / E45 Error Codes

1. **Reduce load size** – If the drum was heavily loaded, remove items to reduce weight. Wash large heavy items (blankets, rugs) separately.
2. **Reset the washer** – Unplug for 60 seconds. After reset, run a small, balanced load and monitor for error recurrence.
3. **Listen for unusual sounds** – Grinding, humming without spinning, or squealing during operation indicate mechanical motor issues.
4. **Check for drum obstructions** – Items like underwire, coins, or small toys can become lodged between the drum and tub, adding mechanical resistance to the motor.

### When to Call a Professional

Motor, inverter board, and bearing replacement require accessing internal washer components. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for professional motor system diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Codes E60–E64 – Door Lock Errors

**SEO Title:** Monogram Washer Error Codes E60–E64 – Door Lock Errors | Causes & Fixes

### What Do Monogram Washer Error Codes E60–E64 Mean?

The E60 through E64 error codes on a Monogram washing machine all relate to **door lock system failures**. Every Monogram front-load washer uses an electronic door lock that must engage before a wash cycle can begin. This safety feature prevents the door from opening during operation. When the control board cannot verify that the door lock has properly engaged or disengaged, these error codes are triggered.

- **E60** – Door lock system fault
- **E61** – Door lock actuator error
- **E62** – Door lock contact/electrical error
- **E63** – Door lock timeout
- **E64** – Door unlock failure

### Common Causes of Door Lock Error Codes

- **Failed door lock actuator** – The electronic actuator that mechanically locks and unlocks the door can burn out.
- **Broken door latch or strike** – Physical damage to the door latch or the frame strike prevents proper lock engagement.
- **Loose wiring to the door lock** – Wiring connections between the door lock assembly and the control board can loosen.
- **Warped or misaligned door** – If the door has been damaged or is out of alignment, the lock pin cannot reach the sensor properly.
- **Debris in the door seal** – Foreign objects caught in the rubber boot seal can prevent the door from fully closing.

### How to Troubleshoot Door Lock Error Codes

1. **Check door closure** – Open and firmly re-close the washer door until you hear a solid click. Push on the door to confirm it is fully seated.
2. **Inspect the door seal** – Look around the rubber boot seal for trapped clothing, debris, or buildup preventing full closure.
3. **Examine the door latch** – Check the plastic latch hook and strike for cracks, chips, or misalignment.
4. **Reset the washer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry.
5. **Listen for lock engagement** – When you press Start, listen for clicking from the door lock area. Repeated clicking without locking suggests actuator failure.

### When to Call a Professional

If door lock errors persist after basic troubleshooting, the door lock assembly likely needs replacement. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for professional door lock diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Codes E66 / E67 – Temperature Sensor Malfunction

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/washer/e66-e67-temperature-sensor-malfunction/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Washer Error Codes E66/E67 – Temperature Sensor | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram washer showing E66 or E67 error? This indicates a temperature sensor malfunction. Learn causes and how to fix E66/E67 on your Monogram washing machine.

**Keywords:** Monogram washer error code E66, Monogram washer E67, Monogram washing machine temperature sensor, Monogram washer thermistor fix, Monogram washer repair

### What Do Monogram Washer Error Codes E66 and E67 Mean?

The E66 and E67 error codes on a Monogram washing machine indicate a **temperature sensor (thermistor) malfunction**. The temperature sensor monitors water temperature in the wash tub, allowing the control board to regulate heating elements (on models with internal heaters) and to verify that hot water from the supply is within the expected temperature range. When the sensor provides readings outside the expected range, these error codes are triggered.

### Common Causes of the E66 / E67 Error Codes

- **Failed temperature sensor (thermistor)** – The thermistor's internal resistance element has failed, producing readings outside the expected range.
- **Disconnected thermistor wiring** – The sensor connector has come loose from the control board or the sensor itself.
- **Thermistor contaminated** – In some cases, detergent buildup on the sensor element can affect its readings.

### How to Troubleshoot the E66 / E67 Error Codes

1. **Reset the washer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry a wash cycle. A temporary electronic glitch can cause false thermistor readings.
2. **Test hot water temperature** – Run hot water at a sink near the washer. If the hot water is extremely hot (above 140°F) or cold, this could affect readings.
3. **Check thermistor resistance** – A technician can test the thermistor's resistance with a multimeter. The expected resistance varies with temperature but should follow a predictable curve.

### When to Call a Professional

If E66 or E67 persists, the thermistor likely needs replacement. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for professional thermistor diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Code UE / UB – Unbalanced Load

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/washer/ue-ub-unbalanced-load/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Washer Error Code UE/UB – Unbalanced Load | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram washer showing UE or UB error? This indicates an unbalanced load during spin. Learn how to fix unbalanced load errors on your Monogram washing machine.

**Keywords:** Monogram washer error code UE, Monogram washer UB error, Monogram washing machine unbalanced load, Monogram washer not spinning, Monogram washer repair

### What Does Monogram Washer Error Code UE or UB Mean?

The UE or UB error code on a Monogram washing machine indicates an **unbalanced load** during the spin cycle. The washer's vibration sensors detect when the laundry load has collected on one side of the drum during spin, creating excessive vibration. Monogram washers will attempt to redistribute the load automatically (by adding water and tumbling) up to several times before displaying the UE or UB code.

This is one of the most common washer error codes and is almost always caused by laundry loading rather than a mechanical failure.

### Common Causes of UE / UB Error Codes

- **Single heavy item** – Washing one large, heavy item alone (bath towel, jeans, blanket) creates an unbalanced load that cannot be distributed evenly.
- **Mixed heavy and light items** – Very heavy and very light items together cause heavy items to clump together.
- **Tangled items** – Sheets or duvet covers can tangle around each other, forming a heavy mass on one side of the drum.
- **Overloaded drum** – An overfull drum prevents free movement and balanced distribution during spin.
- **Washer not level** – A washer that is not sitting perfectly level on all four feet has reduced tolerance for load imbalance.
- **Worn shock absorbers** – Worn suspension dampers reduce the machine's ability to handle even slight imbalances.

### How to Troubleshoot UE / UB Error Codes

1. **Pause and redistribute** – Open the door and redistribute laundry evenly around the drum. Break up tangled clumps. Close the door and restart the spin.
2. **Add or remove items** – If washing a single heavy item, add two or three similar-weight towels. If the drum is overstuffed, remove some items.
3. **Check washer leveling** – Place a level on top and adjust the front leveling feet until the washer is level in both directions. Ensure all four feet make firm contact with the floor.
4. **Run a Spin Only cycle** – After redistributing, run a Spin Only or Drain & Spin cycle to complete spinning.

If UE/UB continues with properly loaded and level machines, the suspension rods, shock absorbers, or vibration sensors may need inspection. Contact our Monogram washer repair team for mechanical inspection and repair. Schedule your Monogram washer repair appointment today.
