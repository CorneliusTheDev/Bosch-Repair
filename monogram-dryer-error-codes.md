### What Does Monogram Dryer Error Code 000 Mean?

The 000 code on a Monogram dryer indicates that **no errors or fault codes have been stored** — the self-diagnostic cycle completed without finding any recorded faults. This code appears when you manually enter the dryer's diagnostic mode and the control board reports that no fault codes are currently stored in memory.

On most Monogram dryers, the diagnostic mode is accessed by pressing a specific button combination described in the owner's manual (or service manual). The display cycles through stored error codes. When 000 appears, it confirms the dryer's electronic systems have no active or stored faults.

This is a normal and positive result. If your dryer is behaving abnormally but shows 000, the problem may be mechanical (such as a worn drum seal, belt, or pulley) rather than electronic.

### How to Use the Diagnostic Mode

1. **Access diagnostic mode** – Follow the button sequence for your specific Monogram dryer model (consult the service manual). Most involve holding specific button combinations at power-on.
2. **Record all codes** – Cycle through all fault codes. Note any codes other than 000.
3. **Exit diagnostic mode** – Follow the exit procedure or simply unplug the dryer.

No repair is needed for a 000 code result. If problems persist, contact our Monogram dryer repair team for mechanical inspection.

## Error Codes 001 / 003 – Defective Inlet Thermistor

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/001-003-defective-inlet-thermistor/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Dryer Error Codes 001/003 – Defective Inlet Thermistor | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram dryer showing 001 or 003 error? This indicates a defective inlet air thermistor. Learn causes and how to fix these codes on your Monogram dryer.

**Keywords:** Monogram dryer error code 001, Monogram dryer error code 003, Monogram dryer inlet thermistor, Monogram dryer temperature sensor, Monogram dryer repair

---

### What Do Monogram Dryer Error Codes 001 and 003 Mean?

Error codes 001 and 003 on a Monogram dryer indicate a **defective inlet thermistor** — the temperature sensor located on the air inlet duct (where air enters the dryer drum). The inlet thermistor measures the temperature of air entering the drum and provides this data to the control board to regulate heating cycles.

- **001** – Inlet thermistor resistance is out of range (typically open circuit or shorted)
- **003** – Similar inlet thermistor fault with slightly different diagnostic parameters

When these errors appear, the dryer may refuse to run a heated cycle, may overheat, or may fail to dry clothes properly.

### Common Causes of the 001 / 003 Error Codes

- **Failed inlet thermistor element** – The thermistor's internal resistance element has failed from age, heat exposure, or mechanical damage.
- **Loose or disconnected thermistor wires** – The wiring connecting the thermistor to the control board has come loose from the connector.
- **Damaged thermistor wiring** – Wires routed near hot components can have insulation damage, creating shorts or opens.
- **Thermistor exposed to excessive heat** – A restricted airflow (clogged lint filter, blocked exhaust duct) can cause temperatures at the sensor location to exceed the sensor's rated limits.

### How to Troubleshoot the 001 / 003 Error Codes

1. **Reconnect loose wires** – Access the inlet thermistor (located on the air inlet duct near the front or back of the drum assembly) and ensure its wiring connector is firmly seated.
2. **Check the lint filter** – Clean the lint filter thoroughly. A clogged lint filter restricts airflow and can cause overheating that damages sensors.
3. **Check the exhaust duct** – Inspect the exhaust duct from the dryer to the exterior vent for blockages, kinks, or bird's nests. Poor exhaust flow can cause extreme temperatures at the inlet sensor.
4. **Test thermistor resistance** – A technician can test the thermistor resistance with a multimeter. Replace if the reading is outside the expected range for the ambient temperature.
5. **Reset the dryer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry after cleaning the lint filter.

### When to Call a Professional

If 001 or 003 persist after lint filter cleaning and checking connections, the inlet thermistor requires replacement. Contact our Monogram dryer repair team for professional thermistor replacement using factory-certified Monogram parts.

### What Do Monogram Dryer Error Codes 002 and 004 Mean?

Error codes 002 and 004 on a Monogram dryer indicate a **defective outlet thermistor** — the temperature sensor located on the blower housing or exhaust duct (where air exits the dryer). The outlet thermistor measures the temperature of air leaving the drum and provides data to the control board to optimize drying efficiency.

- **002** – Outlet thermistor reading out of expected range
- **004** – Similar outlet thermistor fault variant

### Common Causes of the 002 / 004 Error Codes

- **Failed outlet thermistor** – The sensor element has failed electrically.
- **Disconnected thermistor connector** – The wiring harness to the sensor has come loose at the blower housing.
- **Extremely clogged exhaust system** – A completely blocked exhaust system can create abnormally high temperatures at the outlet sensor, damaging it.
- **Damaged wiring** – Thermistor wiring routed near the drum or heating element can become damaged.

### How to Troubleshoot the 002 / 004 Error Codes

1. **Reconnect loose wires** – Check the wiring connector at the outlet thermistor on the blower housing and ensure it is firmly seated.
2. **Clean the exhaust system** – Use a dryer duct cleaning kit to thoroughly clean the exhaust duct from the dryer to the exterior. A clogged duct is a frequent cause of outlet sensor damage.
3. **Test thermistor resistance** – A technician can test the thermistor's resistance to determine if it has failed.
4. **Reset the dryer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry after addressing any blockage.

### When to Call a Professional

If codes persist after checking connections and cleaning the exhaust, the outlet thermistor requires replacement. Contact our Monogram dryer repair team for professional thermistor replacement using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Code 005 – Main Control Board Failure

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/005-main-control-board-failure/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Dryer Error Code 005 – Control Board Failure | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram dryer showing 005 error? This indicates the main control board has failed. Learn causes and how to fix the 005 error code on your Monogram dryer.

### What Does Monogram Dryer Error Code 005 Mean?

The 005 error code on a Monogram dryer indicates a **main control board failure**. The main control board is the electronic brain of the dryer — it manages all functions including heating cycles, motor operation, cycle timing, sensor readings, and display communication. When the control board itself fails or detects an unrecoverable internal error, it records the 005 fault code.

### Common Causes of the 005 Error Code

- **Power surge damage** – Voltage spikes can damage sensitive electronic components on the control board.
- **Component failure on the board** – Individual capacitors, relays, or processors on the board can fail from age or manufacturing defects.
- **Excessive heat exposure** – A severely restricted exhaust system can cause extreme internal temperatures that damage the control board.
- **Water or moisture damage** – In rare cases, moisture from a laundry area plumbing leak can reach the control board.

### How to Troubleshoot the 005 Error Code

1. **Unplug for 3 minutes** – A longer power cycle than normal allows the control board's capacitors to fully discharge. After 3 minutes, restore power and check if the code clears.
2. **Check the exhaust system** – Ensure the dryer has proper airflow to prevent overheating.
3. **Check for power surge history** – If a recent storm or power event occurred, this may have damaged the board.

### When to Call a Professional

The 005 error almost always requires control board replacement. The board must be correctly matched to your Monogram dryer model. Contact our Monogram dryer repair team for professional control board diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified Monogram parts.

### What Does Monogram Dryer Error Code 006 Mean?

The 006 error code on a Monogram dryer indicates that **a control panel button is stuck or continuously pressed**. The control board monitors all panel buttons and if any button appears to be held down for an abnormally long period (or indefinitely), the 006 code is triggered as a safety measure to prevent unintended dryer operation.

### Common Causes of the 006 Error Code

- **Physically stuck button** – A button has physically become stuck in the depressed position from a damaged mechanism, warped panel, or foreign object behind the button.
- **Worn or failed button contact** – A button's internal electrical contact can fail in the closed (pressed) position, permanently signaling "pressed" to the control board.
- **Moisture or contamination under the panel** – Liquid entering the control panel can create short circuits that mimic a stuck button.

### How to Troubleshoot the 006 Error Code

1. **Push each button** – Firmly press and release each button on the control panel one at a time. A physically stuck button may release with firm pressure.
2. **Unplug and clean** – Unplug the dryer. Clean around the edges of the control panel with a slightly damp cloth to remove any residue that may be causing a button to stick.
3. **Reset the dryer** – After cleaning, restore power and check if the code clears.
4. **Inspect for visible damage** – Look for any cracked, dented, or depressed buttons on the panel.

### When to Call a Professional

If the 006 error persists, the control panel or individual button component needs replacement. Contact our Monogram dryer repair team for professional control panel diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Code 007 – Miswired Power Supply

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/007-miswired-power-supply/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Dryer Error Code 007 – Miswired Power Supply | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram dryer showing 007 error? This indicates a miswired power supply connection. Learn causes and how to fix the 007 error on your Monogram dryer.

**Keywords:** Monogram dryer error code 007, Monogram dryer miswired power supply, Monogram dryer 007 fix, Monogram dryer wiring error, Monogram dryer repair

---

### What Does Monogram Dryer Error Code 007 Mean?

The 007 error code on a Monogram dryer indicates a **miswired power supply** — specifically, the Line 2 and Neutral connections on the dryer's terminal block have been swapped or are incorrectly wired. Electric dryers in North America use a 240V power supply with two hot legs (L1 and L2) and a neutral wire. If L2 and Neutral are swapped at the terminal block, the dryer's control board detects the wiring error and displays 007.

This is an installation error that must be corrected at the dryer's terminal block. **This is an electrical safety issue — do not attempt to operate the dryer with a 007 error.**

### Common Causes of the 007 Error Code

- **Incorrect wiring during installation** – The installer swapped the L2 and Neutral positions on the terminal block when connecting the power cord.
- **Power cord changed without following wiring diagram** – When changing from a 3-prong to 4-prong cord (or vice versa), incorrect terminal assignments.
- **Damaged or incorrectly labeled wiring** – Wires that are not clearly color-coded leading to incorrect terminal assignments.

### How to Fix the 007 Error Code

1. **Disconnect power** – Turn off the circuit breaker for the dryer. Do NOT work on the terminal block with power connected.
2. **Access the terminal block** – Remove the rear panel access cover for the terminal block.
3. **Correct the wiring** – Consult the wiring diagram inside the dryer (typically on the back panel sticker) and the power cord wiring diagram. Ensure L1, L2, and Neutral are connected to the correct terminals.
4. **Restore power and retest** – After correcting the wiring, restore power and check that the 007 code no longer appears.

### When to Call a Professional

Electrical wiring should only be performed by qualified personnel. If you are not comfortable working with 240V electrical connections, contact our Monogram dryer repair team or a licensed electrician for correct power supply wiring.

## Error Codes 008 / 00D – Door Switch Malfunction

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/008-00d-door-switch-malfunction/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Dryer Error Codes 008/00D – Door Switch Malfunction | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram dryer showing 008 or 00D error? This indicates a door switch problem. Learn causes and how to fix door switch errors on your Monogram dryer.

**Keywords:** Monogram dryer error code 008, Monogram dryer 00D, Monogram dryer door switch, Monogram dryer door sensor, Monogram dryer repair

---

### What Do Monogram Dryer Error Codes 008 and 00D Mean?

Error codes 008 and 00D on a Monogram dryer indicate a **door switch malfunction** — the door switch is not providing a proper signal to the control board. The door switch is a safety device that detects whether the dryer door is open or closed. The dryer will not run (and should not run) with an open door. If the door switch fails, the dryer may refuse to start even with the door fully closed.

### Common Causes of the 008 / 00D Error Codes

- **Failed door switch** – The mechanical switch has worn out and no longer makes proper electrical contact when the door is closed.
- **Loose wiring to the door switch** – The wiring connector at the switch has come loose.
- **Broken door strike** – The plastic piece on the door that presses the switch button has broken, so the switch doesn't physically activate when the door closes.
- **Door misalignment** – A door that is out of alignment may not press the switch sufficiently.

### How to Troubleshoot the 008 / 00D Error Codes

1. **Check door closure** – Open and firmly re-close the dryer door. Listen for the click of the latch engaging.
2. **Inspect the door strike** – Look at the door's latch area for broken or damaged plastic components.
3. **Check wiring connections** – If accessible, verify the wiring harness connector at the door switch is firmly seated.
4. **Reset the dryer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry.

### When to Call a Professional

If door switch errors persist, the switch or its wiring requires replacement. Contact our Monogram dryer repair team for professional door switch diagnosis and replacement using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error Code 009 – Drive Motor Problem

**Page URL:** `/error-codes/dryer/009-drive-motor-problem/`

**SEO Title:** Monogram Dryer Error Code 009 – Drive Motor Problem | Causes & Fixes

**Meta Description:** Monogram dryer showing 009 error? This indicates a drive motor problem. Learn causes and how to fix the 009 error on your Monogram dryer.

**Keywords:** Monogram dryer error code 009, Monogram dryer motor problem, Monogram dryer 009 fix, Monogram dryer drum not turning, Monogram dryer repair

---

### What Does Monogram Dryer Error Code 009 Mean?

The 009 error code on a Monogram dryer indicates a **drive motor problem** — the motor that rotates the dryer drum and drives the blower wheel has a fault. The control board monitors motor operation through current sensors and tachometer inputs. When the motor draws abnormal current, fails to start, or provides unexpected speed readings, the 009 fault is recorded.

### Common Causes of the 009 Error Code

- **Failed drive motor** – The motor's windings have burned out or its bearings have seized.
- **Loose motor wiring** – The wiring harness connector to the motor has come loose, creating intermittent or no power delivery.
- **Broken or worn drive belt** – If the drive belt has broken, the motor runs unloaded (no drum rotation). While some dryers detect this as a belt error, others may record it as a motor anomaly.
- **Faulty motor start capacitor** – Some dryer motors use a start capacitor. A failed capacitor prevents the motor from starting.
- **Seized drum bearing or support** – If the drum bearing has seized, the motor struggles to rotate the drum and may overheat and fail.

### How to Troubleshoot the 009 Error Code

1. **Listen for motor sounds** – Start a cycle and listen. A hum without drum rotation suggests a seized motor or broken belt. Complete silence suggests no power to the motor.
2. **Check motor wiring** – Reconnect any loose wiring connectors on the motor if accessible.
3. **Check the drive belt** – Access the dryer drum area and verify the drive belt is intact and properly seated on the drum, motor pulley, and idler pulley.
4. **Reset the dryer** – Unplug for 60 seconds and retry.

### When to Call a Professional

Motor replacement requires full disassembly of the dryer cabinet. Belt replacement is more accessible but still requires some disassembly.
