## Error Code – Start Light Flashing / Cycle Interrupted

### What Does Monogram Dishwasher Start Light Flashing Mean?

A flashing Start light on a Monogram dishwasher indicates that **the dishwasher door was opened during an active wash cycle or the cycle was otherwise interrupted**. Monogram dishwashers use a cycle interrupt detection system — when the control board loses the door-locked signal during an active cycle, it pauses the cycle and causes the Start indicator to flash, alerting you that the cycle has been interrupted and needs to be restarted.

This is the most common status indicator you will see on Monogram dishwashers. It is not a component malfunction — it is a cycle state notification that requires a simple user action to resolve.

This behavior appears on Monogram built-in dishwashers, including fully integrated and semi-integrated panel-ready models in the Monogram lineup.

### Common Causes of Start Light Flashing

- **Door opened during wash cycle** – The most common cause. Opening the dishwasher door during any phase of the wash cycle (wash, rinse, dry) causes the cycle to pause and the Start light to flash.
- **Power interruption** – A brief power fluctuation or outage during a cycle can cause the control board to lose cycle state and display the interrupted cycle indicator.
- **Door latch not fully engaged** – If the door latch does not fully engage when the dishwasher door is closed, the control board may interpret this as a door-open event and pause the cycle.
- **Child lock activated** – On models with a child lock feature, accidentally activating the child lock during a cycle can pause the cycle.

### How to Fix the Flashing Start Light

1. **Press the Start pad once** – Press the Start/Resume button once on the control panel.
2. **Close the door within 4 seconds** – Immediately close and fully latch the dishwasher door within 4 seconds of pressing Start.
3. **The cycle will resume** – The dishwasher will resume the interrupted cycle from where it left off (or restart, depending on the model and how long it was paused).
4. **If the cycle cannot resume** – If the paused cycle cannot resume (e.g., the water drained during a long interruption), select the desired wash cycle and press Start normally to begin a new cycle.

### When to Call a Professional

If the Start light flashes immediately when a new cycle begins (even without the door being opened), or if the dishwasher does not resume after pressing Start and closing the door, there may be a door latch or control board issue. ## Error Code – Beeping Once Per Minute / Door Left Open

### What Does Monogram Dishwasher Beeping Once Per Minute Mean?

If your Monogram dishwasher is **beeping once per minute**, this indicates that the dishwasher door was opened or left open during active operation. This is a safety and reminder alert — the dishwasher is telling you that it cannot complete its cycle because the door is open or unlatched.

Monogram dishwashers will not operate with an open door as water could escape and steam could cause burns. The once-per-minute beep is a persistent reminder to close the door.

### Common Causes of the Beeping Alert

- **Door left open after opening during a cycle** – If you opened the door during a wash cycle to add a dish and did not fully close it, the once-per-minute beeping will continue.
- **Door not fully latched** – The door appears closed but has not clicked into a fully latched position. This can happen if dishware near the door edges is preventing full closure.
- **Failed door latch** – A worn or broken door latch may prevent proper engagement, causing the control board to read the door as open even when physically closed.
- **Door gasket interference** – A displaced or damaged door gasket can prevent the door from sealing completely.

### How to Stop the Beeping

1. **Press Start** – Press the Start button on the control panel.
2. **Close the door firmly** – Push the door until you hear or feel a solid click of the latch engaging.
3. **The beeping will stop** – Once the door is latched with Start pressed, the beeping will cease and the cycle will resume.

### When to Call a Professional

If beeping persists even with the door firmly latched, or if you cannot get the door to latch properly, the door latch assembly may need inspection or replacement. Contact our Monogram dishwasher repair team for professional latch diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Monogram parts.

## Error – LEAK DETECTED Display

### What Does Monogram Dishwasher "LEAK DETECTED" Mean?

The "LEAK DETECTED" message on a Monogram dishwasher display indicates that the dishwasher's **built-in leak detection system has found water in a location where it should not be** — typically in the drip tray at the bottom of the dishwasher. Monogram built-in dishwashers are equipped with a leak detection float switch in the base pan. When water accumulates in this tray (from an internal leak), the float rises and triggers the leak detection circuit.

When LEAK DETECTED appears, the dishwasher automatically stops the cycle, shuts off the water inlet valve, and activates the drain pump to minimize potential water damage.

**Do not ignore this alert** — an unaddressed leak can cause water damage to your cabinetry and flooring.

### Common Causes of LEAK DETECTED

- **Deteriorated door gasket** – The rubber door seal around the dishwasher door opening can crack or stiffen with age, allowing water to leak past it during wash cycles.
- **Loose or cracked internal hoses** – Hoses connecting the wash pump to the spray arms, the drain pump to the sump, or the inlet valve to the tub can loosen at clamps or develop cracks.
- **Failed door latch seal** – A compromised seal around the door latch area can allow water to leak past the door.
- **Overfilling from a faulty inlet valve** – A water inlet valve that fails to close completely can overfill the tub, causing water to overflow into the base pan.
- **Cracked sump or tub** – In older units, the plastic tub or sump (bottom of the dishwasher) can develop hairline cracks.
- **Clogged drain causing overflow** – A severely clogged drain can cause water to back up and overflow into the base pan.

### How to Troubleshoot LEAK DETECTED

1. **Stop using the dishwasher** – Do not run the dishwasher again until the leak source is identified and repaired to prevent further water damage.
2. **Unplug or cut power** – Turn off power to the dishwasher at the circuit breaker.
3. **Check the door gasket** – Open the door and carefully inspect the entire perimeter of the door gasket for cracks, tears, hardening, or areas where the gasket has separated from its groove.
4. **Check under the dishwasher** – Use a flashlight to look under the dishwasher for any visible drips or water trails on the floor.
5. **Inspect drain hose connections** – Check the drain hose connection at the sink drain or garbage disposal for leaks.
6. **Allow the base pan to dry** – Even after fixing the leak source, the float switch in the base pan will keep the LEAK DETECTED message active until the accumulated water evaporates or is removed. The base pan must be dry before normal operation resumes.

### When to Call a Professional

All but the simplest leak sources (such as a loose external drain hose connection) require professional diagnosis and repair. Internal hose replacements, gasket replacement, inlet valve replacement, and sump repair require partial or full dishwasher disassembly. Contact our Monogram dishwasher repair team for leak diagnosis and repair using factory-certified Monogram parts. Do not delay — water damage from a leaking dishwasher can escalate quickly.

### What Does Monogram Dishwasher End-of-Cycle Beeping Mean?

The beeping at the end of a Monogram dishwasher cycle is **normal operation** — it is the end-of-cycle completion signal indicating that the wash cycle has finished successfully. Monogram dishwashers are designed to provide an audible notification when the cycle is complete so you know the dishes are ready to unload.

This is not an error code. However, the sound can be adjusted or disabled on most Monogram dishwasher models.

### How to Adjust the End-of-Cycle Beeping

On most Monogram dishwasher models, the end-of-cycle beeping can be toggled on or off using a control panel button sequence:

1. **Make sure the dishwasher is not running** – Ensure no cycle is active.
2. **Press the Dry Options button** – Press the Dry Options pad (or Heated Dry button, depending on your model) **5 times within 3 seconds**.
3. **The sound setting will toggle** – The dishwasher will indicate (via a beep or display change) whether the end-of-cycle sound is now ON or OFF.
4. **Test the setting** – Run a short cycle to verify the sound setting has changed.

Note: The exact button combination may vary by model. Consult your Monogram dishwasher owner's manual for the specific procedure for your model number.

If your Monogram dishwasher is beeping at unexpected times — not at the end of a cycle — this may indicate a fault code or sensor alert that requires professional diagnosis.
